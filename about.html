About Us

Our Mission, Vision and Impact

Empowering wildlife conservation through technology, education, and community action.

Who We Are

WildGuard Society is a non-profit organization dedicated to protecting Tanzania's iconic wildlife and ecosystems through cutting-edge technology and community-driven initiatives.

Our Mission

To leverage technology and community engagement to protect wildlife, preserve habitats, and foster a world where humans and nature coexist in harmony.

Our Vision

A thriving ecosystem where technology empowers communities to become guardians of their natural heritage, ensuring wildlife flourishes for generations to come.

Our Impact

Over 10,000 animals monitored, 50+ rangers trained, and 20 communities engaged in active conservation programs across East Africa.

Core Technology

The tools we use to monitor and protect wildlife

AI Monitoring

Machine learning models analyze camera trap images and acoustic sensors for real-time wildlife detection and threat identification.

Mesh Networks

Low-power IoT sensor networks provide continuous data from remote locations without relying on cellular infrastructure.
