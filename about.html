About the project

Protect. Learn. Inspire.

Wildlife Explorer is a conservation-first learning experience built to feel like a premium field journal while remaining simple enough to use across desktop and mobile.

Mission

Connect people to living ecosystems

We bring together visual storytelling, quick facts, and real wildlife media to create a site that feels credible, cinematic, and easy to explore.

  • Conservation learning
  • Field-style storytelling
  • Responsive access
What we do
  • Showcase organisms Use real photography and short editorial summaries to keep every page grounded in actual species.
  • Support learning Pair visuals with habitat, behavior, and conservation notes for students and curious visitors.
  • Keep it usable Maintain a clean layout that works on a phone, tablet, or desktop without losing visual impact.
    • Vision

    A digital sanctuary for Kenya's wildlife story

    The design direction keeps the National Geographic mood but uses Kenya wildlife cues, stronger contrast, and more structured cards for a polished editorial finish.
