Adopt an Animal

Symbolically adopt a wildlife ambassador and fund its protection, habitat, and care — every adoption powers real conservation on the ground.

How Adoption Works

Three simple steps between you and a wild friend

1. Choose a Species

Pick a wildlife ambassador from our protected parks and learn how it is monitored and cared for by our field teams.

2. Make Your Gift

Your symbolic adoption funds anti-poaching patrols, habitat restoration, veterinary care, and community conservation programs.

3. Receive Updates

Get a personalised adoption certificate plus field updates and stories about your adopted animal throughout the year.
