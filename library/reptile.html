Reptiles

Sun-loving lizards, secretive snakes and ancient crocodilians.

Reptiles of East Africa

Ancient survivors from crocodiles to chameleons across diverse habitats.

Flap-necked Chameleon

Least Concern

Scientific Name: Chamaeleo dilepis

Weight Range: 0.5 - 1 oz (15 - 30 g)

Key Detail: Can move eyes independently and change color for communication, temperature regulation, and camouflage.

Leopard Tortoise

Least Concern

Scientific Name: Stigmochelys pardalis

Weight Range: 29 - 88 lbs (13 - 40 kg)

Key Detail: Fourth largest tortoise species; beautiful leopard-like spots on domed shell fade with age.

Nile Crocodile

Least Concern

Scientific Name: Crocodylus niloticus

Weight Range: 500 - 1,650 lbs (225 - 750 kg)

Key Detail: Africa's largest freshwater predator; can hold breath underwater for up to 2 hours.

Black Mamba

Least Concern

Scientific Name: Dendroaspis polylepis

Weight Range: 2.2 - 3.5 lbs (1 - 1.6 kg)

Key Detail: Africa's longest venomous snake; highly aggressive when cornered with neurotoxic venom.

Puff Adder

Least Concern

Scientific Name: Bitis arietans

Weight Range: 2.2 - 4.4 lbs (1 - 2 kg)

Key Detail: Responsible for most snakebite fatalities in Africa; relies on camouflage rather than fleeing.

Rhino Viper

Least Concern

Scientific Name: Bitis nasicornis

Weight Range: 2 - 3 lbs (0.9 - 1.4 kg)

Key Detail: Striking geometric patterns with horn-like scales on snout; potent hemotoxic venom.

Nile Monitor

Least Concern

Scientific Name: Varanus niloticus

Weight Range: 13 - 26 lbs (6 - 12 kg)

Key Detail: Africa's longest lizard; excellent swimmer and climber, raids crocodile nests for eggs.

Savannah Monitor

Least Concern

Scientific Name: Varanus exanthematicus

Weight Range: 10 - 15 lbs (4.5 - 6.8 kg)

Key Detail: Popular in pet trade; powerful limbs and tail for digging burrows and defense.

Rainbow Agama

Least Concern

Scientific Name: Agama agama

Weight Range: 0.3 - 0.5 oz (8 - 15 g)

Key Detail: Males display brilliant blue heads and red-orange bodies during breeding season.

Brown House Snake

Least Concern

Scientific Name: Boaedon fuliginosus

Weight Range: 0.5 - 1.5 lbs (0.2 - 0.7 kg)

Key Detail: Non-venomous constrictor commonly found around human settlements hunting rodents.

African Rock Python

Near Threatened

Scientific Name: Python sebae

Weight Range: 70 - 120 lbs (32 - 55 kg)

Key Detail: Africa's largest snake; non-venomous constrictor capable of swallowing antelope whole.

Speke's Hinged Tortoise

Vulnerable

Scientific Name: Kinixys spekii

Weight Range: 2 - 4 lbs (1 - 2 kg)

Key Detail: Unique hinge in shell allows it to close the rear portion for protection against predators.
