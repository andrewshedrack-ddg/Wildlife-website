Wildlife Categories

Mammals

Warm-blooded vertebrates from mighty elephants to tiny bats, inhabiting every continent.

Birds

Feathered vertebrates ranging from tiny sunbirds to majestic eagles across every habitat.

Reptiles

Sun-loving lizards, secretive snakes and ancient crocodilians.

Amphibians

Frogs and toads adapted to varied habitats from rainforests to deserts.

Aquatic Life

Plants

Fungi

Bacteria

Microorganisms contributing to digestion, nitrogen fixation and more.

Viruses

Microscopic infectious particles that replicate inside living cells of animals, plants, and bacteria.

How It Works

Three simple steps to become a conservation guardian

1. Scan

Capture any animal, plant, or ecosystem indicator using your camera or an uploaded photo. Our AI recognizes and identifies it.

2. Learn

Get instant field guide data: species details, conservation status, habitat, habits, and audible read-aloud narration.

3. Protect

Your observations help us track species sightings, detect threats, and focus conservation efforts where they're needed most.
