Aquatic Life of East Africa

Rich freshwater ecosystems from the Great Lakes to coastal rivers and wetlands.

Nile Perch

Least Concern

Scientific Name: Lates niloticus

Conservation Status: Least Concern

Weight Range: Up to 440 lbs (200 kg)

Key Detail: Massive predator introduced to Lake Victoria; caused extinction of hundreds of endemic cichlid species.

Nile Tilapia

Least Concern

Scientific Name: Oreochromis niloticus

Conservation Status: Least Concern

Weight Range: Up to 9.9 lbs (4.5 kg)

Key Detail: One of world's most important aquaculture species; mouthbrooders protecting eggs.

African Lungfish

Least Concern

Scientific Name: Protopterus annectens

Conservation Status: Least Concern

Weight Range: Up to 8.8 lbs (4 kg)

Key Detail: Can breathe air and estivate in mud cocoons for years during drought; living fossil.

Electric Catfish

Least Concern

Scientific Name: Malapterurus electricus

Conservation Status: Least Concern

Weight Range: Up to 44 lbs (20 kg)

Key Detail: Generates up to 350 volts to stun prey and defend; ancient Egyptian depictions known.

Bayad Catfish

Least Concern

Scientific Name: Bagrus bajad

Conservation Status: Least Concern

Weight Range: Up to 33 lbs (15 kg)

Key Detail: Large predatory catfish with long whiskers; important food fish across Nile basin.

African Tigerfish

Least Concern

Scientific Name: Hydrocynus forskahlii

Conservation Status: Least Concern

Weight Range: Up to 33 lbs (15 kg)

Key Detail: Ferocious predator with razor-sharp teeth; known for spectacular aerial attacks on birds.

Featherfin Squeaker

Least Concern

Scientific Name: Synodontis eupterus

Conservation Status: Least Concern

Weight Range: Up to 1.5 lbs (0.7 kg)

Key Detail: Upside-down swimmer with long feathery dorsal fin; produces squeaking sounds.

Victoria Carp

Critically Endangered

Scientific Name: Labeo victorianus

Conservation Status: Critically Endangered

Weight Range: Up to 11 lbs (5 kg)

Key Detail: Endemic to Lake Victoria; decimated by Nile Perch introduction and habitat degradation.

Burton's Haplochromis

Least Concern

Scientific Name: Astatotilapia burtoni

Conservation Status: Least Concern

Weight Range: Up to 0.2 lbs (100 g)

Key Detail: Model organism for cichlid evolution; mouthbrooder with complex social behaviors.

Elephantfish

Least Concern

Scientific Name: Mormyrus kannume

Conservation Status: Least Concern

Weight Range: Up to 2.2 lbs (1 kg)

Key Detail: Weakly electric fish using electrolocation; elongated snout probes muddy bottoms for invertebrates.

Nile Bichir

Least Concern

Scientific Name: Polypterus bichir

Conservation Status: Least Concern

Weight Range: Up to 7.7 lbs (3.5 kg)

Key Detail: Living fossil with armor-like scales and multiple dorsal finlets; can breathe air.

African Sharptooth Catfish

Least Concern

Scientific Name: Clarias gariepinus

Conservation Status: Least Concern

Weight Range: Up to 130 lbs (59 kg)

Key Detail: Can breathe air and "walk" on land between water bodies; incredibly hardy survivor.
