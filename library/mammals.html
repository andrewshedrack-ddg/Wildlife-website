Panthera leoPredator

Lion

Social carnivore that depends on healthy prey populations and connected grassland habitats.

  • Prides are family groups
  • Most active at dawn
Loxodonta africanaHerbivore

Elephant

A keystone species that reshapes landscapes, opens trails, and supports biodiversity.

  • Uses trunk for feeding
  • Strong memory and family bonds
Giraffa camelopardalisBrowser

Giraffe

Kenya’s tall icon, shaped for browsing treetops and spotting movement across open country.

  • Longest neck of any land mammal
  • Feed on acacia and shrubs
Equus quaggaGrazing

Plains Zebra

Distinctive striped grazer that helps define the motion and texture of the savannah landscape.

  • Lives in family groups
  • Stripes are unique
Acinonyx jubatusSprinter

Cheetah

Built for speed and agility, with a more fragile conservation profile than most people realize.

  • Fastest land animal
  • Uses keen eyesight to hunt
Diceros bicornisEndangered

Black Rhino

A powerful browser that remains one of Africa’s most closely protected species.

  • Uses hooked lip to feed
  • Strong conservation priority
