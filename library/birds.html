Birds

Over 1,100 bird species thrive in diverse ecosystems worldwide.

Birds of East Africa

Diverse avian species from raptors to waterbirds across savannas, wetlands, and forests.

African Fish Eagle

Least Concern

Scientific Name: Haliaeetus vocifer

Weight Range: 4.4 - 7.7 lbs (2 - 3.5 kg)

Key Detail: Known for its iconic call that echoes across African waterways, often called the 'voice of Africa'.

White-headed Vulture

Critically Endangered

Scientific Name: Trigonoceps occipitalis

Weight Range: 8.8 - 15.4 lbs (4 - 7 kg)

Key Detail: Distinctive white head and pink face; populations declining rapidly due to poisoning and habitat loss.

Lesser Flamingo

Near Threatened

Scientific Name: Phoeniconaias minor

Weight Range: 2.6 - 4.4 lbs (1.2 - 2 kg)

Key Detail: Millions gather at East African soda lakes, creating one of nature's most spectacular pink horizons.

Peacock

Least Concern

Scientific Name: Pavo cristatus

Weight Range: 8.8 - 13.2 lbs (4 - 6 kg)

Key Detail: Male's iridescent tail feathers can span 5 feet, used in elaborate courtship displays.

Verreaux's Eagle-owl

Least Concern

Scientific Name: Ketupa lactea

Weight Range: 3.5 - 6.6 lbs (1.6 - 3 kg)

Key Detail: Africa's largest owl with distinctive pink eyelids and powerful talons capable of taking small antelopes.

Egyptian Goose

Least Concern

Scientific Name: Alopochen aegyptiaca

Weight Range: 2.2 - 6.6 lbs (1 - 3 kg)

Key Detail: Ancient Egyptians considered them sacred; now common across sub-Saharan wetlands and grasslands.

Secretary Bird

Endangered

Scientific Name: Sagittarius serpentarius

Weight Range: 5 - 9.5 lbs (2.3 - 4.3 kg)

Key Detail: Hunts on foot, stomping snakes and rodents with powerful legs; named for quill-like head feathers resembling clerks.

Grey Crowned Crane

Endangered

Scientific Name: Balearica regulorum

Weight Range: 6.6 - 8.8 lbs (3 - 4 kg)

Key Detail: Uganda's national bird with golden crown feathers; performs elaborate dancing displays during courtship.

Ostrich

Least Concern

Scientific Name: Struthio camelus

Weight Range: 139 - 320 lbs (63 - 145 kg)

Key Detail: World's largest and fastest bird on land, reaching 43 mph (70 km/h); lays the largest eggs of any bird.

Kori Bustard

Near Threatened

Scientific Name: Ardeotis kori

Weight Range: 15 - 40 lbs (7 - 18 kg)

Key Detail: Africa's heaviest flying bird; males perform spectacular balloon displays inflating their necks.

Marabou Stork

Least Concern

Scientific Name: Leptoptilos crumenifer

Weight Range: 9 - 20 lbs (4 - 9 kg)

Key Detail: Scavenger with massive bill and throat pouch; often seen near human settlements and carcasses.

Southern Ground Hornbill

Vulnerable

Scientific Name: Bucorvus leadbeateri

Weight Range: 5 - 13.6 lbs (2.3 - 6.2 kg)

Key Detail: Cooperative breeders living in family groups; deep booming calls can be heard 3 km away.
