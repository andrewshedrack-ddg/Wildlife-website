Plants

From savanna grasses to tropical rainforest canopies.

Plants of East Africa

Iconic trees, flowers, and grasses shaping Africa's diverse landscapes.

Baobab Tree

Least Concern

Scientific Name: Adansonia digitata

Conservation Status: Least Concern

Height: Up to 98 ft (30 m)

Key Detail: "Tree of Life" - stores thousands of liters of water; fruit rich in vitamin C; some specimens over 1,000 years old.

Umbrella Thorn Acacia

Least Concern

Scientific Name: Vachellia tortilis

Conservation Status: Least Concern

Height: Up to 69 ft (21 m)

Key Detail: Iconic flat-topped silhouette of African savannas; deep taproots access groundwater.

Whistling Thorn

Least Concern

Scientific Name: Vachellia drepanolobium

Conservation Status: Least Concern

Height: Up to 20 ft (6 m)

Key Detail: Swollen thorns house aggressive ants that defend tree; wind through thorns creates whistling sound.

African Myrrh

Least Concern

Scientific Name: Commiphora africana

Conservation Status: Least Concern

Height: Up to 16 ft (5 m)

Key Detail: Produces aromatic resin (myrrh) used since ancient times for incense, medicine, and perfume.

Candelabra Tree

Least Concern

Scientific Name: Euphorbia candelabrum

Conservation Status: Least Concern

Height: Up to 49 ft (15 m)

Key Detail: Succulent tree with toxic latex; traditional uses include fish poison and medicinal applications.

Strangler Fig

Least Concern

Scientific Name: Ficus thonningii

Conservation Status: Least Concern

Height: Up to 65 ft (20 m)

Key Detail: Begins life as epiphyte, sends roots down to strangle host tree; keystone species for wildlife.

Cape Aloe

Least Concern

Scientific Name: Aloe ferox

Conservation Status: Least Concern

Height: Up to 10 ft (3 m)

Key Detail: Bitter sap harvested for laxative harvested for laxative and cosmetic uses; spectacular orange-red flower spikes.

Bird of Paradise

Least Concern

Scientific Name: Strelitzia reginae

Conservation Status: Least Concern

Height: Up to 6.5 ft (2 m)

Key Detail: Iconic crane-like flowers; South Africa's floral emblem; pollinated by sunbirds.

King Protea

Least Concern

Scientific Name: Protea cynaroides

Conservation Status: Least Concern

Height: Up to 3.3 ft (1 m)

Key Detail: South Africa's national flower; largest flower head in Protea genus; survives fires via underground rootstock.

Red Hot Poker

Least Concern

Scientific Name: Kniphofia uvaria

Conservation Status: Least Concern

Height: Up to 5 ft (1.5 m)

Key Detail: Dramatic torch-like flower spikes; nectar-rich attracting sunbirds and insects.

Red Oat Grass

Least Concern

Scientific Name: Themeda triandra

Conservation Status: Least Concern

Height: Up to 4.9 ft (1.5 m)

Key Detail: Dominant savanna grass; fire-adapted; critical forage for wildebeest, zebra, and other grazers.

African Orchid

Vulnerable

Scientific Name: Aerangis africana

Conservation Status: Vulnerable

Height: Up to 12 in (30 cm)

Key Detail: Epiphytic orchid with fragrant white flowers; threatened by habitat loss and illegal collection.
