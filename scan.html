How It Works

Three steps to identify and learn about wildlife

1. Capture

Open your camera or upload a photo. Our AI instantly begins processing the image with MobileNet neural network technology.

2. Identify

Receive species identification, scientific name, conservation status, habitat details, and ecological role in seconds.

3. Contribute

Your scans help build our wildlife database. Every observation strengthens conservation efforts across Africa and beyond.
