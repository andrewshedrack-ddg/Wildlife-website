We Are WildGuard Society

Protecting the Wildlife Heritage and The Beauty Of The Nature

We Are The WildGuard Guardians working alongside, Parks, Reserves, farmers, herders, and village rangers. We scan wildlife, track threats, and look after the land our families have lived on for generations.

Our Protected Parks

Nature's most iconic reserves and biodiversity hotspots

Serengeti National Park

Home to the Great Migration — 1.5 million wildebeest cross endless plains each year seeking fresh grazing.

Ngorongoro Crater

World's largest intact volcanic caldera — a natural sanctuary for over 25,000 large mammals.

Tarangire National Park

Iconic elephant herds and ancient baobab trees. A birdwatcher's paradise with 550+ species.

Selous Game Reserve

Africa's largest wilderness — 50,000 km² of untouched rivers, woodlands, and savannas.

How It Works

Three steps to join the conservation movement

1. Scan

Use your camera or upload a photo. Our AI instantly identifies the species and retrieves field guide data.

2. Learn

Get habitat details, conservation status, population trends, and voice narration that reads the guide aloud.

3. Protect

Your observations help track populations, detect threats, and guide conservation across Tanzania and beyond.
