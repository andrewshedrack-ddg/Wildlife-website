Youth-Led Environmental Conservation

We Are The Guardians of Nature

WildGuard Society brings together young people to understand environmental changes and take meaningful action for a healthier planet.

Discover Our Mission

Who We Are

A youth led environmental conservation movement responding to environmental changes that affect nature and the balance of ecosystems.

Our Purpose

As the world experiences increasing challenges such as pollution, habitat degradation, biodiversity loss, climate change, unsustainable resource use, and ecosystem disturbances, we bring young people together to understand these changes and take meaningful action.

Youth Collaboration

We empower individuals to become active guardians of nature and contributors to a healthier planet through conservation, environmental education, sustainability initiatives, and innovation.

Our Belief

Protecting nature requires more than awareness; it requires observation, adaptation, action, and collective effort. We transform passion into action for a sustainable future.

Magnificent Wildlife

Home to some of Africa's most incredible species

Lion

Vulnerable - A male's darker, thicker mane indicates top health and higher testosterone.

Elephant

Endangered - Their massive ears act like built-in air conditioners, cooling down blood.

Buffalo

Near Threatened - An adult male's horns fuse together forming a solid bone shield known as a 'boss'.

Leopard

Vulnerable - Leopards can haul prey weighing as much as themselves up into tree branches.

Rhinoceros

Critically Endangered - They have a hooked, prehensile upper lip used like a thumb to strip leaves.

National Parks

Explore protected wilderness areas around the world

Serengeti National Park

A UNESCO World Heritage Site hosting the largest overland wildlife migration on earth.

Ngorongoro Conservation Area

The world's largest intact volcanic caldera, sheltering over 25,000 large mammals.

Tarangire National Park

Famous for iconic Baobab trees and high concentrations of elephants.

Lake Manyara National Park

Diverse park featuring an alkaline lake filled with pink flamingos.

Ruaha National Park

A hidden gem with dramatic Great Ruaha River scenery.
