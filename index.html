How It Works

Three simple steps to become a conservation guardian

1. Scan

Capture any animal, plant, or ecosystem indicator using your camera or an uploaded photo. Our AI recognises and identifies it.

2. Learn

Get instant field guide data: species details, conservation status, habitat, habits, and audible read-aloud narration.

3. Protect

Your observations help us track species distributions, detect threats, and focus conservation efforts where they're needed most.
