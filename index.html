Our Protected Parks

Explore the world's most iconic and biodiverse reserves

Serengeti National Park

Home to the Great Migration — over 1.5 million wildebeest — and some of Africa's most iconic wildlife.

Ngorongoro Crater

World's largest intact volcanic caldera — a natural enclosure for over 25,000 large animals.

Tarangire National Park

Massive elephant herds gathering among ancient baobab trees. One of Africa's best birdwatching destinations.

Selous Game Reserve

Africa's largest protected wilderness area — covering 50,000 km² of rivers, woodland, and grassland.

Conservation in Action

Real initiatives protecting wildlife and habitats across East Africa

Anti-Poaching Patrols

Award-winning ranger teams patrol critical corridors 24/7, using advanced monitoring and rapid-response logistics to protect endangered species from poachers.

Habitat Restoration

Reforesting critical wildlife corridors to reconnect fragmented habitats. Over 50,000 native trees planted since our launch.

Community Engagement

Empowering local communities as conservation stewards through education, sustainable workshops, and direct involvement at every reserve.

AI-Powered Monitoring

Real-time species detection and threat alerts using camera-floor neural networks deployed across thousands of square kilometres.

How It Works

Three simple steps to become a conservation guardian

1. Scan

Capture any animal, plant, or ecosystem indicator using your camera or an uploaded photo. Our AI recognizes and identifies it.

2. Learn

Get instant field guide data: species details, conservation status, habitat, habits, and audible read-aloud narration.

3. Protect

Your observations contribute to global species tracking, threat detection, and targeted conservation efforts where help is needed.
