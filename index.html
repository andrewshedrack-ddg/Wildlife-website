Savannah Grasslands

Lions are the only cats that live in groups, called prides, which are family units that can include up to three males, a dozen females, and their young.

King of Beasts Vulnerable
Elephant

Largest Land Mammal Endangered

Protected Ecosystems

Serengeti
Serengeti National Park
UNESCO World Heritage

Home to the world's greatest wildlife spectacle - the Great Migration of over 1.5 million wildebeest, 250,000 zebras, and countless predators following the rains.

14,750 km² Est. 1951
Ngorongoro
Ngorongoro Conservation Area
Volcanic Caldera

Features the world's largest inactive volcanic caldera, forming a natural enclosure for an incredible density of wildlife including the densest lion population in Africa.

8,292 km² Est. 1959
Tarangire
Tarangire National Park
Baobab Landscape

Famous for its high density of elephants and ancient baobab trees, offering spectacular wildlife viewing during the dry season when animals gather along the Tarangire River.

2,850 km² Est. 1970
Selous
Selous Game Reserve
Largest Protected Area

One of the largest faunal reserves in the world, encompassing diverse habitats from miombo woodlands to open grasslands, supporting incredible biodiversity including wild dogs.

50,000 km² Est. 1922

Our Mission

WildGuard Explorer combines cutting-edge AI technology with traditional conservation wisdom to protect Tanzania's wildlife heritage. Through real-time monitoring, predictive analytics, and community engagement, we're creating a sustainable future where both wildlife and local communities thrive.
