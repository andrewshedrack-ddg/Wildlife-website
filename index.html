Magnificent Wildlife

Home to some of Africa's most incredible species

Lion

Vulnerable - A male's darker, thicker mane indicates top health and higher testosterone, helping them attract mates and intimidate rivals.

Elephant

Endangered - Their massive ears act like built-in air conditioners, cooling down blood to drop their body temperature.

Buffalo

Near Threatened - An adult male's horns fuse together at the base, forming a solid bone shield known as a 'boss'.

Leopard

Vulnerable - Leopards can haul prey weighing as much as themselves entirely up into tree branches to protect from scavengers.

Rhinoceros

Critically Endangered - They have a hooked, prehensile upper lip used like a thumb to strip leaves and twigs from branches.

National Parks

Explore Tanzania's protected wilderness areas

Serengeti National Park

A UNESCO World Heritage Site hosting the largest overland wildlife migration on earth, with over 1.5 million wildebeest and zebra.

Ngorongoro Conservation Area

The world's largest intact volcanic caldera, sheltering over 25,000 large mammals including rare black rhinos.

Tarangire National Park

Famous for iconic Baobab trees and high concentrations of elephants gathering around the Tarangire River during dry season.

Lake Manyara National Park

Diverse park featuring an alkaline lake filled with pink flamingos, dense forests, and legendary tree-climbing lions.

Ruaha National Park

Tanzania's hidden gem with dramatic Great Ruaha River scenery, huge buffalo herds, and exceptionally large lion prides.
